# Recorder Modded Songs
**Recorder Modded Songs** (stylized as **\[ST] Recorder Modded Songs** in-game) is a resource- and data pack that adds [jukebox song definitions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jukebox_song_definition) and [recorded disc styles](/Stancements/Docs/Recorded%20Disc%20Style.md) for several modded songs.

The data pack is always enabled by default, both on new and existing worlds.

![](/Melony%20Studios%20Wiki/Resource%20Packs/Screenshots/recorder_modded_songs.png)

> *"Record modded songs using the Music Recorder"*
>
> — `pack.mcmeta` description.

All songs that are ==highlighted== represent *recorded disc styles*, and not jukebox songs.
